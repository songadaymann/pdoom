THE BOSS · ALL THREE
1 SHIPS (tennis): one Recognizer, then two, then three. They shoot; click as a shot flashes in front of you to hit it back. 2 SHIELD DOWN (volley): it fires at you (dodge) while glowing bits light up on its face; aim at one and everyone throws with you. 3 THE TRICK SHOT: one window left, round the back. Bank your disc off five glowing marks: the camera rides it, time slows near each mark, and you steer it in with the mouse. Press 1, 2 or 3 to jump to a part (or open play.html?m=boss&part=3). One disc, yours. It bounces off pillars, walls and the shield, then flies back to your hand; no second throw until you catch it. Bank it through the gap. The twenty throw on their own. B · TENNIS
The MCP can only shoot through its own gap. Click as the shot reaches you to hit it back; it flashes white in the sweet spot. Rallies speed up and hit harder. Sometimes it serves to one of the twenty. C · CROWD VOLLEY
The spec's model. The twenty stand on your side of the ring and throw when you throw. You choose where to stand and when to call it; sinks and posts come at them.
