## For the voice generator

p(DOOM) is a Doom mod. The player is a frontier-AI CEO (Sam Altman or Dario Amodei) rescuing AI-safety researchers from levels run by rogue AIs. Level 4 is a Matrix-style human pod farm run by AGENTIC SMITH, an Agent Smith look-alike AI agent who materialises out of any camera that sees you and possesses the people you free. Five researchers (Jaan Tallinn, Nick Bostrom, Daniel Kokotajlo, Katja Grace, Yoshua Bengio) are freed one puzzle each and gather in the refuge; the way out is its ringing payphone. Tone: Doom-terse and deadpan; the researchers get the last laugh, not the punchline. SMITH is measured, nasal and contemptuous, never loud; the jailer arm is a flat, cheerful call-centre machine.

**Files.** One WAV per line, named exactly by its key: `<key>.wav` (for example `jaan-hint.wav`). Say the line as written; if a word changes while recording, the game's subtitle is updated to match.

**The guide's lines.** Whoever you free first becomes the guide, so each of the five has the same `-freed-guide`, `-safe`, `-allsafe` and `-camera` lines. Record all five: only one plays in a given game.
