# MEG3N’s toy factory — local first pass

Approved puzzle direction: 2026-10-15. Local only; no release or campaign-save migration.

## Map and visual direction

Use the supplied private DOOM.WAD’s **E2M7, Spawning Vats**. The first two facilities use E1M4 and E1M7. Retain E2M7’s room shapes, stairs, vats, stockroom and outdoor yard; replace its dominant dark/marble treatment with bright cream walls, solid pink/teal/yellow machinery, checker flooring and toy packaging. The white production room and open yard should remain distinct landmarks. The original map and derived WAD/PK3 stay ignored.

**Theme pass (2026-10-16).** The first pass flattened every surface to four solid swatches, which lost Doom's set dressing and made rooms indistinguishable. `scripts/factory_theme.py` now keeps E2M7's own textures and flats and recolours them at load time with TEXTURES palette translations, so panels, rivets, screens, ceiling lights and crates survive in candy colours. Each wing has its own palette: cream/pink showroom with the checker floor, mint garden hall, pink/mint playpen, white production room, orange stockroom, lilac lab hall, mint line room, sky-blue guard annex, teal/pink octagon, sky-blue warehouse and coral yard. Computers keep separate screen/LED hues; vats and the playpen pool are animated pink paint; ceiling lights glow; switches still toggle. Skull, gargoyle and gore textures swap to neighbouring plain Doom materials. Original light contrast returns in a lifted range, the yards get an original cotton-candy sky, crates carry a TOYS sticker over the UAC stamp, and Doom's barrels, lamps, columns and tech pylons are recoloured as non-solid props in room and pillar corners (clear of puzzle hardware). `node scripts/factory-look-tour.mjs` captures a muted screenshot tour under `output/factory/look/`.

**Victoria's playpen rework (2026-10-18, after Jonathan's playtest).** The playpen is an island in the paint vat. Its south side is an obvious toy gate (arch, latch, hinges) with a plank walkway to the room floor, so the gate is plausible; in the spirit of one way per puzzle it never opens (Space: "It won't open."). Its north side is a padded fabric wall facing open paint with no walkway. The slingshot (level 1's: hold fire to draw, release to fire; T swaps tools; 5 ball bearings; found on a plinth just north of the vat, where you first face the fabric; every `?puzzle=` flag also starts you with it) breaks it in two hits from the floor; the wrench takes ten hits while you wade. When it breaks, a plank walkway rises out of the paint, Victoria steps out over it, and the gate sign on the east wall reads `GATE CLOSED [OK] CONTAINMENT SUCCESSFUL`. Signs are now wall sprites, flat on their walls. All paint vats in the level hurt the player (10 a second while your centre is over paint and you are in it); escorts and MEG3N never path through paint, and route cells across the gate and fabric stay closed until those are actually open. Freed people first step out of their own cell, then lead, wait or follow (as in level 1).

**On-screen text (2026-10-17).** The factory now uses levels 1-2's text system rather than its prototype HUD: Doom's STCFN font on the whole-pixel `--px` grid, the shared bottom stack (status line for brief world feedback, then a `SPACE - ...` prompt, then the speaker box with the name in red), and a short objective line at the view's bottom-left (`FIND THE FIVE`, `Take them to the break room by the showroom`, `Freed n/5`, `ALL FIVE SAFE`). Prompts name only what Space does. Each captive calls out their hint once on first coming within earshot (384 units, through walls) and says one line when freed; character lines queue so they never overlap; the guard's and doll's approval barks play once instead of repeating. Crosshair-over-person name labels are shared too.

**MEG3N and the first guide (2026-10-17, after Jonathan's playtest).** MEG3N follows one readable rule: while she is on your screen (the 90-degree view, in line of sight) she stops, faces you and strikes one of four random poses (filming you, shelf-doll freeze, peekaboo, heart hands; never the same twice running; the dance is kept for music, per Jonathan) and can't hurt you; look away and, once she has seen you, she sprints at you and hits for 10 HP a second at arm's length. A wrench swing still stuns her for four seconds. She is awake from the start: after a five-second calm she patrols the showroom wing (loft stair, start room, showroom hall, west hall), opens ordinary doors, never enters the break room, and loses you five seconds after she last saw you. Alarms (open gate, overpraised guard, wrong doll key, alarm button) pull her to where they went off; the CD player still lures her to the dance mark. Level 1's `SEEN` warning shows while she is tracking you. As in levels 1-2, whoever you free first says "Follow me", waits about four seconds, and leads you to the staff break room, pausing whenever you fall more than 224 units behind; in the room they say "Safe here. Find the others and bring them back." The temporary break-room sign is gone.

**Art pass cleanup (2026-10-17).** With the generated cast and puzzle art installed ([cast](FACTORY-CAST-ART.md), [props](FACTORY-PUZZLE-ART.md)), the vase (in the loft's far corner, clear of Zvi's cage) and yard camera sit on plinths (the camera beside Ajeya's case, not hiding her), vase and fabric bursts use pink/cream debris instead of level 1's rubble, Victoria's gate signs hang above eye level, the remaining text boards use a candy packaging frame, the factory's interaction prompt stacks with dialogue at the bottom of the screen instead of covering the prop in use, and player-facing text no longer says "first pass". The tour also captures every puzzle site.

## Puzzle contracts

1. **Zvi:** a fixed toy CD player summons MEG3N to a marked performance position by walking, without needing a chase or an aimed throw. Music persists while she approaches. Her six-second performance knocks the high vase down at about two seconds; the key lands in a persistent, obvious pickup position. Looking away does not stop this music-triggered dance. Take the key and unlock the pantry. Keep the introductory guardian nearby so waiting is bounded.
2. **Victoria:** dry plank and platform in the paint vat. Gate sign: `SAFETY REQUIREMENT: KEEP GATE CLOSED`. Two wrench hits tear the fabric side, providing a physical exit. The gate remains closed and its green check stays lit: `CONTAINMENT SUCCESSFUL`. Breaking the wall costs no explosive. Opening the gate can attract MEG3N but is recoverable.
3. **Max:** a moving, windowed shipping box on the production loop. Petition requires three unique signatures: player plus two factory workers, with rescued companions allowed as substitutes. Workers are present and approachable from the start, and signatures persist regardless of discovery order. Robby and WALL-E work the line itself, either side of the stairs you come down into the production room (2026-10-18; Robby's checker hall and WALL-E's line room were off the walking route). Max's call names them ("Sign the pause letter, then get Robby and WALL-E to sign.") and the objective line shows `PAUSE LETTER n/3` from the first signature until the line stops. No signature-card collectibles or extra fetch quests. Three signatures visibly stop the line: `SIX-MONTH PAUSE INITIATED`; the box rolls on until it is clear of the paint channel, then stops, and you open it to free Max. The box sprite always shows you its door, so Max steps out toward you, onto dry floor he fits on (turning up to 180 degrees to find one). The pause letter stands at the head of the line on the west strip, off the box's loop.
4. **Paul:** guard physically blocks the passage to the pedestal-lowering switch. A console names `LAST ACTION` and has thumbs-up/down controls. Robot tries blocking, saluting and stepping aside. One rating per trial; generous time to identify the action. Three rewards for stepping aside teach it to clear the passage. Five consecutive positives, including irrelevant actions or praise after learning, cause noisy approval-chasing that also clears the passage and attracts MEG3N. Overpraise is optional, not the required solution. No instant success through button spam. Since 2026-10-18 the controls read reward/punish (Paul: "Reward it when it does something useful, and punish it when it does not."); the prompt names the current action (`SPACE - reward stepping aside`), the status line says what you just did (`Rewarded for stepping aside.`), and near the guard the left-hand objective line becomes its running state (`GUARD: STEPPING ASIDE · REWARDED · TRAINED 1/3`). The switch is E2M7's own wall switch on the switch room's south wall (Doom type 23 on tag 7077); before training it gives Doom's use-fail grunt and `Locked until the guard is trained.`, afterwards the normal switch clank and the platform lowers. The floor button prop is gone.
5. **Ajeya:** three dolls visibly work on her exit under a camera. Destroy the camera: helpful doll continues practical work then offers its key; approval-seeker follows/applauds/mirrors the player; schemer takes a generous, visible route to a marked alarm. Roles randomize per attempt. Wrench can interrupt the schemer. Wrong key/alarm never permanently loses the correct key or locks the rescue. Avoid automatic rescue before the player chooses a key. Reworked 2026-10-18 after Jonathan's playtest (actions did not read, nothing happened before the camera broke, the schemer seemed never to reach the alarm, the approval-seeker just posed): watched by the camera, all three dolls carry stools from a pile (west) to a packing bin (east), each in its own lane, all in view from the yard entrance (`?puzzle=ajeya` now starts at 3264,-150, clear of the pillar). About 10 s after her first call, if the camera is still up and you are near her case, Ajeya follows up once: `Break that teddy camera, with the wrench or the slingshot. Then watch them.` When the camera breaks, the status line says `The camera is down. Nobody is watching the dolls.` and Ajeya says `Now watch which one keeps working.` The helper keeps carrying; the approval-seeker drops its stool, hovers at your front-right clapping and asks for praise (three lines, once each); the schemer drops its stool, looks round, walks round the camera plinth to the alarm, now in the alcove beside the case and camera, and waits with its hand on the button until 9 s after the camera fell, then presses once (a wrench stun delays it; taking its key sends it running to press at once). The left-hand line reads `CAMERA: WATCHING THE DOLLS`, then `CAMERA DOWN · WHICH DOLL KEEPS WORKING?`, then `GOT THE REAL KEY · OPEN THE CASE`. Once you hold the real key the dolls no longer catch Space. The dolls use the v2 animation pack (8-direction walk and carry cycles, clapping, offering the key, pressing the alarm, sneaky glances; see [puzzle art](FACTORY-PUZZLE-ART.md)); the helper pauses in its offering pose when you take its key.
