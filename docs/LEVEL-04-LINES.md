# AGENTIC SMITH's Pod Farm: every spoken line

All 78 lines in level 4, by speaker, generated by `python3 scripts/agentic-lines.py --write` from the game's own line list (checked against `doom-mod/actors/Agentic*.zs`). The game shows each one as a subtitle in the speaker box; a recorded take saved as `<key>.wav` (import with `scripts/import-voice-lines.py`) plays in its place, from where the speaker stands. What still needs recording is in [LEVEL-04-VOICE-TODO.md](LEVEL-04-VOICE-TODO.md).

## Context

p(DOOM) is a Doom mod. The player is a frontier-AI CEO (Sam Altman or Dario Amodei) rescuing the AI-safety researchers who warned them, from levels run by rogue AIs. Level 4 (THY FLESH COMPUTED, E2M2 south half) is a Matrix-style human pod farm run by AGENTIC SMITH, an Agent Smith look-alike AI agent: cameras are his eyes, he materialises at any camera that sees you, and he possesses the people you free. Five researchers are held one machine each (a payphone booth, a pod and the white Construct, a harvest pod behind crushers, an island in a red pool, a test rig). The first one freed leads you to the refuge, the warm exit room across the nukage bridge; SMITH will not enter it. The way out is the refuge payphone. Tone: Doom-terse and deadpan; the researchers get the last laugh, not the punchline.

**Voices.** Jaan, Nick, Daniel, Katja and Yoshua are real people. On 2026-10-23 Jonathan voiced all 78 lines with his local Breeze TTS voice lab: SMITH and the five researchers from saved reference-conditioned profiles (the researchers from public speech excerpts, SMITH from an Agent Smith film clip), the jailer arm from an original designed voice (the factory guard's from level 3). As for level 3, that decision supersedes the spec's text-only rule for real people for this batch. The takes are local, ignored media (ASSETS.md section 4); without them the game shows subtitles. Where a take's words differ from the script, the table and the subtitle show what is said (`jaan-cut`). SMITH and the jailer arm play as machines (voice-over.js `MODES`), placed where they stand.

**The guide's lines.** Whoever you free first becomes the guide, so each of the five has the same `-freed-guide`, `-safe`, `-allsafe` and `-camera` lines. Only the speaker's plays in a given game.
