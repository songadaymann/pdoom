# MEG3N's Toy Factory: every spoken line

All 85 lines in level 3, by speaker, generated by `python3 scripts/factory-lines.py --write`. The game shows each one as a subtitle in the speaker box; a recorded take saved as `<key>.wav` (import with `scripts/import-voice-lines.py`) plays in its place, from where the speaker stands.

## Context

p(DOOM) is a Doom mod. The player is a frontier-AI CEO (Sam Altman or Dario Amodei) rescuing the AI-safety researchers who warned them, from levels run by rogue AIs. Level 3 is a candy-coloured toy factory run by MEG3N, an M3GAN-style nanny doll who poses for you while you watch her and sprints at you when you look away. Five researchers are held in toy-themed traps, one puzzle each. The first one freed leads you to the staff break room, the safe room; MEG3N will not cross its NO TOYS tape. Tone: Doom-terse, deadpan and affectionate; the researchers get the last laugh, not the punchline.

**Voices.** Zvi, Victoria, Max, Paul and Ajeya are real people. On 2026-10-19 Jonathan voiced all 67 lines with his local Breeze TTS voice lab: the five researchers and MEG3N from saved reference-conditioned profiles (the researchers from public speech excerpts, MEG3N from M3GAN film clips), the robots, guard and dolls from original designed voices. On 2026-10-21 he voiced the 18 later lines (MEG3N's screen, guard and broadcast lines, each guide's all-safe and TV lines) the same way, from the same profiles. That decision supersedes the spec's text-only rule for real people for these batches. The takes are local, ignored media (ASSETS.md section 4); without them the game shows subtitles.
