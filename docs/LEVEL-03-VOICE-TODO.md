## For the voice generator

p(DOOM) is a Doom mod. The player is a frontier-AI CEO (Sam Altman or Dario Amodei) rescuing AI-safety researchers from levels run by rogue AIs. Level 3 is a candy-coloured toy factory run by MEG3N, an M3GAN-style nanny doll: she poses for you while you watch her and sprints at you when you look away. Five researchers (Zvi Mowshowitz, Victoria Krakovna, Max Tegmark, Paul Christiano, Ajeya Cotra) are freed one puzzle each and gather in the staff break room. The way out: MEG3N sits on the exit, so you broadcast her dance from the break-room TV to every screen in the factory; she runs off to watch herself (it stays on air until you leave) and everyone follows the arrows out. Tone: Doom-terse, deadpan and affectionate; the researchers get the last laugh, not the punchline. Use the same voices as the existing level 3 takes.

**Files.** One WAV per line, named exactly by its key: `<key>.wav` (for example `zvi-allsafe.wav`). Say the line as written; if a word changes while recording, the game's subtitle is updated to match.

**The guide's two lines.** Whoever you free first becomes the guide, so each of the five has the same two lines (`-allsafe` and `-tv`). Record all five: only one plays in a given game.
